Orbit

Friction is dissipative, so time reversal is no longer exact: running the clock backwards will not retrace the path it came along. That is the physics, not a bug — leapfrog is exactly reversible only for velocity-independent forces.

Disc orientation

Spin direction matters more than closeness. Set one retrograde and one prograde to get a long tail on one galaxy and almost nothing on the other — that asymmetry, not the closeness of the passage, is the central result of Toomre & Toomre 1972. Measured here: prograde throws 4.7% of a disc beyond 20 kpc, retrograde throws none of 60,000 particles.
