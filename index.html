Orbit

Friction is dissipative, so time reversal is no longer exact: running the clock backwards will not retrace the path it came along. That is the physics, not a bug — leapfrog is exactly reversible only for velocity-independent forces.

Disc orientation

Spin direction matters more than closeness. Set one retrograde and one prograde to get a long tail on one galaxy and almost nothing on the other — that asymmetry, not the closeness of the passage, is the dominant effect in a tidal encounter. Measured here: prograde throws 15.1% of a disc beyond 20 kpc against 2.5% retrograde, a ratio of 6.0x on identical orbits.
